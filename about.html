Our Story

Leading Food Manufacturing Excellence in Egypt & the Arab World

Founded in 1997 in Egypt with a vision to bring families together through delicious food, Kayan Group has become the region's premier food manufacturing company. We combine international quality standards with authentic Egyptian recipes to create products that are loved across Egypt and Arab countries.

Our team of food experts, quality specialists, and production professionals has created over 50 beloved products for families across the Middle East. From our ISO-certified facilities in Tanta and 10th of Ramadan, we deliver exceptional taste and quality that brings smiles to consumers and success to our retail partners.

Our Quality Promise

ISO-Certified Manufacturing Excellence

Our state-of-the-art facilities in Tanta and 10th of Ramadan enable us to deliver premium food products that compete on the international stage. With ISO 22000, 9001, and 45001 certifications, we have everything needed to bring authentic Egyptian flavors to your table with safety and quality.

We specialize in authentic recipes that honor Egyptian traditions while meeting international food safety standards. Our experienced team ensures every product maintains consistent quality across all our markets in Egypt, Iraq, Libya, Sudan, Palestine, and Kuwait.
