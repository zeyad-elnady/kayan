About Kayan Group

Leading Food Manufacturing Since 1997

We create delicious food products that bring families together and sweeten life's moments across the Middle East.

We are a leading food manufacturing company established in 1997 in Egypt, specializing in high-quality chocolates, candies, juices, and snacks for the Middle East market. From our facilities in Tanta and 10th of Ramadan, we create products that combine authentic taste with international quality standards.

Whether you're a distributor looking for fast-moving products, a retailer seeking quality brands, or a family wanting delicious treats — we're here to sweeten your success story with ISO-certified excellence.
